# Demo Script (maximum 3 minutes; desktop screen recording, not a phone)

No recording exists yet. Record at 1440 x 900 or 1920 x 1080 with a screen recorder that exports .webm (for example OBS Studio) and save it as `demo/demo.webm`. Rehearse once; the timings below total 2 min 50 s.

| Time | Screen | Narration (speak in your own words) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:20 | Overview | "PSTA 5 and NST2 aim for a 50% rise in priority crop productivity, measured by NISR's Seasonal Agricultural Survey. In 2025/26, all eight priority crops were below their target nationally: maize at 87%, beans 79%, soybean 52%." |
| 0:20-0:35 | Overview, how to use | "A national gap is closed in districts. Umuzi Analytics answers one planning question: where are district yields persistently below national, and what should be checked first?" |
| 0:35-1:05 | Evidence Map, maize, Season A | "We harmonised five NISR releases. For maize in Season A, 12 districts are below national in all three releases. Ruhango: 1.38 tonnes per hectare against 1.99 nationally, below in 3 of 3 seasons, and both inputs below national, so the route is to check input access." |
| 1:05-1:30 | Persistence | "This is the discovery. District positions barely move between seasons: rank correlations of 0.9 and above. A low district this season was low before. A single-season table hides that." |
| 1:30-1:55 | Input Context | "And most persistent maize shortfalls are not input-poor: 9 of 12 have fertilizer or seed uptake at or above national. Western districts like Ngororero use more fertilizer than the national average but little improved seed. That is a variety, soil or terrain question, not a fertilizer question." |
| 1:55-2:15 | Evidence Brief, then download | "A planner exports this brief: the evidence, the category, field questions and the limits: farmer-reported yields, no district standard errors, inputs pooled across crops." |
| 2:15-2:35 | Data and Methods | "Every number traces to an NISR table and a snapshot. The 2025 Season B release was not available, so it is shown as missing, never as zero." |
| 2:35-2:50 | Terminal: `python -m scripts.refresh_data status` | "When NISR publishes the next season, one command validates it. A bad file is quarantined and the app keeps the last approved data. Thank you." |
